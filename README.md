Skewer's Symbiotic scissors track enemies in flight, and Seamstress's targeting circle shows which enemy they will follow. Skewer stays in the special slot, with its usual sticking and pickup slash. Configure damage for both primaries, both secondaries and Skewer in Risk of Options, along with lifesteal and health costs.

The package icon now appears on the mod's Risk of Options page. Damage sliders use 0-1000% of base damage, with Seamstress's original defaults:

- Trim: 120% claw hits, 150% on the third hit. Separate scissor-hit settings for normal and third hits, both 200%.
- Flurry: 140% claw hits and 200% scissor hits, set separately.
- Clip: 70% per snip. Planar Manipulation: 200% base coefficient, keeping its needle, speed and victim-health calculations.
- Skewer: 400% initial impact and 200% Symbiotic pickup slash, set separately.

Scissor hits still require the matching scissors. Changing damage does not change attack speed, hit counts, proc coefficients or knockback. Planar Manipulation's separate health-based damage remains even with its base coefficient at zero.

The earlier settings are unchanged: It Hungers lifesteal is 1-25% (default 15%), Skewer's health cost is 0-25% (default 15%), and the Retaliate checkbox disables self-bleed damage and needle gain only during its Insatiable effect.

Changes apply to the next attack or damage calculation without restarting. Original skill tooltips can still show the original values.

Tracking looks for visible enemies within 60 metres of the aim direction. The target circle and the fired scissors share the same target, and steering stops when the scissors stick to an enemy or the ground. Shots without a target still fly normally. The original secondary skills and skill slots are unchanged.

Requires the original Seamstress mod and Risk of Options, plus BepInEx. The hooks were checked against both Seamstress 1.6.10 and the newer supplied Seamstress source; this update still needs an in-game test.

For multiplayer, install the same version on every player's profile and use matching settings. Import the ZIP through r2modman's Settings > Profile > Import local mod.
