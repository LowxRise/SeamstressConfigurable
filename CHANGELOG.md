1.2.0

- Added enemy tracking to Skewer's Symbiotic scissors during flight.
- Kept Skewer in the special slot and kept the original secondary skills.
- Tracking stops when the scissors land. Their speed, sticking, pickup slash and health cost are unchanged.
- Kept all existing damage, lifesteal and Retaliate settings.
- Uses the original Seamstress mod, with no additional survivor mod required.

1.1.0

- Added separate claw and scissor damage sliders for Trim and Flurry, including Trim's third claw and third scissor hits.
- Added damage per snip for Clip and a base damage setting for Planar Manipulation's held and thrown impacts.
- Added separate Skewer initial-impact and Symbiotic pickup-slash damage settings.
- New damage settings default to Seamstress's original values and allow 0-2000% base damage. They apply without restarting.
- Scoped the damage patches to the original attacks and scissors.
- Kept existing lifesteal, Retaliate and Skewer health-cost settings unchanged.
- Added the changelog to the Thunderstore package and updated the package description.

- Added configurable It Hungers lifesteal, Retaliate's self-bleed and needle-gain toggle, and Skewer's health cost.
